IMD colour codes

What each alert colour means

Green — No warningAll is well. No action needed, just stay aware.
Yellow — WatchBe updated. Weather may worsen; keep an eye on IMD.
Orange — Be preparedHeavy to very heavy rain or strong winds likely. Prepare to act.
Red — Take actionExtremely bad weather expected. Stay indoors, follow officials.
